02 Why Chief of Staff (over founder / BD / VC), and why Eligo
  • I’m at my best a step behind the leader — making the machine run so the person with the vision can stay on it. And honestly, you’re part of the draw: you didn’t just start a frontier-science company — with Hello Tomorrow, BioWeek and DeepTech Founder, you contribute to built a whole ecosystem to push deep science out of the lab and into the world. Inventing the science and obsessing over getting it to people is exactly the bridge I like to serve, and it’s genuinely inspiring to me. I’ve done the Chief of Staff job twice, for real: VP Strategy & Innovation at Lagardère, then VP Innovation at AXA.
  • Eligo because the mission has real impact, and you’re at the point where the engine room decides whether the science actually reaches patients
  • I won’t pretend to be a biotech person — my healthcare exposure is limited (some healthtech at AXA, a short stint at AP-HP) — but you say no specific background is required, and engine-room ownership is exactly what I’d bring
03 Something tedious I’ve owned and made run better
  • Turning messy, undocumented operations into clean systems people actually use: codifying a field team’s best practices at Lagardère; building the knowledge base and governance behind AXA’s AI use cases
  • The unglamorous reality: naming things, chasing owners, killing duplicates, holding the bar when everyone wants to move on
  • Did I enjoy it? Yes — done well, the system ends up running without me, and that’s the point
04 One thing I’m unreasonably proud of the quality of
  • At Lagardère, over ~4 years, I built the group’s innovation approach from close to a blank page: a community across 10+ countries, a startup-accelerator partnership (Plug & Play), and a ~400-person innovation challenge I ran end to end
  • Not a flashy artifact — but something real that took root and kept running after me
05 One way to improve the posting
  • Honestly? It sells the role brilliantly, but tells me almost nothing about you
  • Being a Chief of Staff means working hand-in-glove with one person — and my conviction is that a great CoS is as much about fit with that person as about the skill set
  • So the one thing I’d add is a bit of you: how you actually work, what energises and frustrates you, how you like to be challenged. That’s how the right person knows they’re the right person — and how the wrong ones opt out early
